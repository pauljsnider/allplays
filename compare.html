Comparison sources and specialist capabilities

Sources reviewed October 28, 2026. Product features and packages vary. This comparison explains why ALL PLAYS works well for everyday team and family workflows; shared features are not exclusive claims.

Hudl: Focus Flex offers automatic camera capture and upload with the required hardware, connection and package. Assist provides video-linked stats and reports by sport and paid package; most sports use human analysts, with AI support in specific sports. Club soccer tools include playlists, comments, drawings, college search and opt-in recruiting. See club packages.

TeamSnap: TeamSnap ONE streaming and Falcon include camera-based video options; hardware is separate and replay/highlight access depends on the package. A free small-team tier is available.

GameChanger: Free team management includes schedules, messaging and RSVPs. Lineup recommendations are a baseball/softball beta, distinct from practice planning. Athlete profiles, career stats and clips are also available.

SportsEngine: Its mobile app includes multi-athlete calendars, guardians, chat and photos. Standalone teams can use the free app; league-managed and HQ packages differ.

Spond: Parents can manage multiple children, with invitations and calendar sync. Posts support comments, reactions and media.

TV viewing is not exclusive to ALL PLAYS: Hudl Fan supports Roku, with access depending on the available content and event. SportsEngine Play describes screen mirroring, which differs from a native Roku app. ALL PLAYS’ Roku capability here is private-install YouTube replay viewing.

ALL PLAYS boundaries: Live scoring, clips, manual highlights, athlete profiles and scouting notes are supported. Managed camera capture/cloud recording and automatic video-to-stats/highlights remain proposed. Roku viewing here means game replays, not a claim of live Roku broadcasting.
